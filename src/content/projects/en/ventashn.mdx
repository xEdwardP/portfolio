The full retail sales cycle: product catalog and inventory, customers, suppliers, cashier
sales with a session-based cart, printable PDF receipts, reports with geographic filters, and
a business-intelligence dashboard with ten interactive charts.

What separates it from a generic POS is that **it is built for a specific context**. Seeded
geography covers Central America and the customer document field is designed for the
**14-digit Honduran RTN**, not an abstract identifier. The interface is Spanish by
configuration, not by translation.

Access is controlled by two roles, `admin` and `cajero`, enforced through Laravel Gates and
custom middleware. It is a classic MVC monolith — controllers, Eloquent models and Blade
views — and that was the right call for the size of the problem.
