Day-to-day operation of a retail business: product catalog and stock, suppliers, purchases,
users, and over-the-counter sales with printable PDF receipts.

The application is organised around two roles, `admin` and `cajero`, enforced by custom
middleware and authorization gates. The detail worth pointing out is that **those same gates
drive the sidebar navigation**: a cashier never sees links that would return a 403. The
permission is a single source of truth, not a rule duplicated between the backend and the
template.

The interface is in Spanish and amounts display in Honduran lempiras.
