A public storefront and an admin back office on the same foundation, organised around a
strict separation of responsibilities.

Controllers stay thin: they neither validate nor decide, they only orchestrate. Validation
lives in dedicated **Form Requests**, and every unit of business logic is encapsulated in a
**reusable service class**.

It is the same idea I apply in the .NET projects with Repository and Unit of Work, expressed
in Laravel's own conventions: the goal is not following a pattern by name, but making sure
that adding a feature never requires reading a three-hundred-line controller to find where to
touch.

Payments go through PayPal Checkout.
