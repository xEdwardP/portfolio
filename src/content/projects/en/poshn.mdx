## The problem

A point-of-sale system needs to keep purchases, stock and amounts consistent while adapting
to local retail needs. An incomplete operation can leave a purchase recorded without its
corresponding inventory entry.

PosHn is a desktop system for Honduras, **under development**, building that foundation
with business rules separated from the interface.

## Architecture decisions

### Four layers with distinct responsibilities

The domain holds entities, value objects and pure business rules. The application layer
coordinates use cases, validations and contracts. Infrastructure implements persistence
with Entity Framework Core and SQL Server, while presentation contains the WPF interface
and dependency configuration.

Dependencies point toward the domain. This allows calculations and validations to be
tested without opening windows or depending on visual components.

### Purchases and inventory as a single operation

Purchase registration validates input, checks products and rejects duplicate invoice
numbers for the same supplier. The purchase and its incoming stock movements are saved
within a single transaction.

Inventory tracks stock by branch, a movement ledger and stock adjustments. Incoming stock
updates the weighted average cost; outgoing stock is validated against available quantities.

### Testable rules

Cost calculations, rounding and validations live in the domain. The project includes xUnit
tests and uses dependency injection to connect use cases with their implementations.

## Current status

The implemented foundation includes purchasing, inventory, cash operations and rules for
fiscal authorizations. Development continues in stages: the invoicing engine and sales
screen are part of the planned work.
