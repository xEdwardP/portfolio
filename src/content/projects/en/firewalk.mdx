Operational back office for retail and distribution businesses, covering the full supply
cycle.

A purchase order is created for a supplier and its line items are added interactively: each
line generates **its own batch**, with its own expiration date and prices. The order is
emailed to the supplier, and once the goods arrive, finalising the purchase registers stock
into a specific branch and writes the matching inventory movement **in a single atomic
operation**.

The result is that no quantity in stock is anonymous: it traces back to its batch, its
supplier, its branch and the purchase that brought it in. That is what makes it possible to
respond to an expiration or a defective batch without auditing the whole inventory.
